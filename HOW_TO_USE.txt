HOW TO USE pipewire_sync  (no programming needed)
==================================================

WHAT THIS DOES
--------------
It sends whatever plays on your laptop to BOTH Bluetooth earbuds at the
same time, and keeps them in sync with each other. Without it, one earbud
usually plays noticeably later than the other.


WHAT YOU NEED
-------------
- Windows 10 or 11 (64-bit)
- A pair of Bluetooth earbuds, connected to the laptop
- The unzipped project folder (with pipewire_sync_win.exe inside)


STEPS
-----
1. Put both earbuds in your ears.

2. Connect them to the laptop:
      Settings  >  Bluetooth & devices
   Both earbuds must say "Connected".

3. Open the project folder and double-click   START_HERE.bat
   (double-clicking   pipewire_sync_win.exe   directly works too -
   whichever you click, do it BEFORE starting the music.)

4. A black window opens. Wait a couple of seconds - it prints
   some lines and then a status block like:

      dev 0 ... latency=  14.0 ms  offset=  +0.00 ms ...

   "offset" close to +0.00 means the two earbuds are in sync.
   That's it - now play music or a video and both earbuds
   play together.

5. To stop: click the window and press   q
   (or just close the window).
   Your speakers and volume are restored automatically.


IMPORTANT TO KNOW
-----------------
- The laptop speakers are MUTED while it runs (that is normal -
  the sound comes from the earbuds instead). Stop the program and
  the speakers come back.

- If Windows shows a blue "Windows protected your PC" screen:
      click  "More info"  then  "Run anyway".
   (The program is not signed, so Windows does not know it yet.)

- Start the program BEFORE the music/video. It resyncs constantly,
  so even if a bud briefly disconnects, it recovers by itself.


IF SOMETHING GOES WRONG
-----------------------
- "No Bluetooth sinks found" / it exits immediately:
    The earbuds were not connected. Connect both in Settings >
    Bluetooth & devices, then double-click START_HERE.bat again.

- You hear the sound on the laptop speakers AND earbuds:
    Normal for some laptops - it means the mute self-test could
    not safely mute them. The sync still works.

- One earbud still lags the others:
    Stop the program (q), then start it again with a small fix.
    For example, if earbud #2 is late by about 15 ms, run:

        pipewire_sync_win.exe 2:15

    (open the folder in File Explorer, type cmd in the address
    bar, press Enter, then type that command.)
    The setting is remembered for next time.

- The window flashes and closes too fast to read:
    Use START_HERE.bat instead of double-clicking the .exe -
    it waits for a keypress so you can read the message.
